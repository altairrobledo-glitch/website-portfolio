A little about me

I’m Alex, a designer who brings strategy, clarity, and a little curiosity to digital products. I partner with thoughtful teams, from the first conversation to the final detail.

I started in graphic design and moved into product work because I wanted to see how design changes what people do every day. I work best in small teams where design, engineering, and research sit close together.

How I work
  • Understand
    Talk with people, map the problem, and agree on what success looks like.
  • Shape
    Sketch, prototype, and test early so ideas fail cheaply.
  • Build
    Design the details and hand off a system the team can grow.
